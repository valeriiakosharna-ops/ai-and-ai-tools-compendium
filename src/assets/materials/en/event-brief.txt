TRAINING SOURCE 1. INITIAL EVENT BRIEF
This is a fictional document for the NotebookLM exercise only.

You are organising a volunteer meeting for your community. You have 7 days to prepare, two helpers, and an initial budget of UAH 3,000.

Plan for 20 people and a meeting lasting up to two hours. Collect and sort books for exchange, prepare short descriptions, and give the collection to a community library.

Divide venue arrangements, invitations, materials, and registration among the three organisers. Do not plan to use special equipment.

The venue is not confirmed. This document contains no address, contact details, or verified prices. Check estimated expenses before buying anything.
